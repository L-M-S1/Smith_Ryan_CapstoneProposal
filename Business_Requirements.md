Nurses should only have access to patient records that they are assigned to. This prevents confusion and reduces chance of error in taking care of one patient to the next.
Doctors should have access to every patient's information, as there is only one Doctor, Nurses patient information can be shared with the doctor.
The Doctor should have pseudo admin permissions on the network.
HR information should be kept separate from patient information.
All employees will have to authenticate who they are via Username and password.

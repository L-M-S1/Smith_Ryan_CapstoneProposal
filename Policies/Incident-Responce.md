# Incident Response

What is an Incident?

An incident is anything that compromises patient data or suspends employee workflow. Ransomware, suspected malware, lost or stolen devices, phishing, wrong recipient emails with confidential information, and unauthorized access are just a few of the incidents that can occur.

Who is responsible?

The owner of the information at risk is responsible for reporting it to their manager or Doctor Hawk.

The process
1. Report immediately. Tell Doctor Hawk or your manager verbally and in writing for documentation of the event. No blame will be given when reporting an incident.

2. Contain. Disconnect affected machine from network in the event of a malware attack. Turn off Wi-Fi Capabilities. DO NOT power off unless there is a physical danger, evidence can get lost. Remove any other potentially infected machines from the network, change passwords, look for the account exposed.

3. Assess. What data was involved? Was the data encrypted, copied, or accessed. Log every decision, timestamp and use a hash for the document. 

4. Eradicate. Remove the threat, patch the entry point, reset credentials. Restore backups only if you can be sure they are free from infection.

5. Notify. Patient notification and regulator reporting within the deadline. HIPPA - 60 days. GDPR 72 hours from awareness. Notify patients in plain language what happened, how they could have been affected, and steps being taken to resolve the issue.

6. Recover. Restore systems (refer to step 3), monitor closely for the first two weeks, make paper scheduling sheets and patient consent forms for offline care if the process takes longer.

7. Lessons Learned. Write down what happened, what worked, what didn't, and what to change in order to ensure this does not happen again or faster response to take.

Things to keep in mind

Never pay a ransom without legal advice.
Preserve evidence, document everything.
Keep physical copies of the incident report.
Assume something is a breach when something looks off until proven otherwise.

Testing

Walk through one scenario every six months. Backups need to be tested quarterly.

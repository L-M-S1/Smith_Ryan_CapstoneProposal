# Acceptable Use Policy

1. Puropose

This policy dictates how staff can use the technology, including but not limited to, Computers, Printers, Tablets, Phones, software, and POS systems. All employees are responsible for following the rules stipulated in this policy, failure to do so will result in disciplinary action.

2. Who is Responsible

Doctor: Responsible for the oversight of Nurse and HR use, access control

All Staff: Responsible for using the systems strictly for work purposes, adhering to any necessary frameworks and all training.

3. Permitted Use

You may only use the systems to carry out work related tasks. Use of personal devices is permitted so long as it does not interfere with your work.

4. Acceptable use requirements

Passwords must be longer than eight characters, include a number, special character, and contain a combination of upper and lowercase letters. Do not share this password with anybody.
Use Multi-factor authentication whenever applicable.
Lock your computer when leaving your workstation
Verify a patients name and birthday and cross reference it with their patient profile. DO NOT DISCUSS PATIENT INFORMATION BEFORE CONFIRMING IDENTITY.
Report any suspicious activity to Doctor Hawk.

5. Prohibited Use

Do NOT

Access patient records unless the patient is speaking to you, or you are perfroming care for the patient. 
Photograph patient records for ANY reason. Patient information should not leave the clinic unless the patient asks to be transferred or their information deleted.
Discuss identifiable patients in public spaces.
Send protected information by unencrypted email or SMS unless the patient has been informed of the risks.
Connect unknown USB drives or hard disks.
Bypass security controls.

6. Monitoring

The clinic may monitor network activity, emails, and system logs for security and compliance. Monitoring is proportional and not used for individualized surveillance, you will be informed if your transmissions are flagged.

7. Leaving the clinic

All work devices are to remain at the clinic. If you leave the clinic permanently your device will be scrubbed and will be continued to be in use. 

8. Breaches and Consequences

Suspected breaches go directly to Doctor Hawk, do not investigate or delete anything. Breaches will be handled on a case by case basis and consequences will be proportional to the breach. This could range from additional training up to a report to law enforcement when required.

9. Training and acknowledgement

All staff must complete data security training when they start and annually as they work. You may not begin your duties until this training is complete.

10. Review of policy

This policy will be reviewed annually or whenever a change is made to our security posture.

Acknowledgement

By signing this document you have read and understand the Acceptable Use Policy and are beholden to any disciplinary actions that arise as a consequence of breaking the agreement.

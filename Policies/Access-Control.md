# Access Control

1. One person, one login. Do not share your login details to anybody, never use someone else's.

2. You will only have access to what is strictly necessary for your role.

3. MFA is required on everything that supports it. Email, software, remote access.

4. Passwords are required to be at least eight characters, containing one number, one special character, and a combination of upper and lower case letters must be used.

5. Access will only be granted after you have signed all required documents, completed all required training, and authorized to work by Doctor Hawk. You will not be permitted access on a temporary account.

6. Admin accounts are only for correcting mistakes, not for reading patient files.

7. Emergency access to another clinician's patient is allowed when necessary but must be documented and signed by Doctor Hawk.

8. Quarterly, your account will be reviewed and once a month your records will be spot checked for any activates that could break any of our policies.

9. If you leave the clinic, your accounts will be disabled the same day.

Doctor Hawk is responsible for approving your access, holds admin permissions, and will conduct the quarterly review.

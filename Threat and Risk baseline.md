Phishing for nurse/doctor credentials. If someone were to gain access to the Doctors credentials the entire system would be compromised. The rotation of credentials and a secure router is the first line of defense for this situation, as well as training for phising awareness.

Malware leading to the opening of ports/peering into files. Malware can lock up employee PCs and the server. Files could be viewed and encrypted. The router being properly configured is the best line of defense, as well as fault tolerant file storage.

Excessive privileges to given to HR. Not too big of an issue, HR department could be trained on HIPPA guidelines which would cause no real problems. Creating a separate role for HR would resolve this.

Misconfiguration. Misconfiguration can cause everything to fail depending on what is wrong. A keen eye and a triple check is what is going to be saving the company with this issue.

Vulnerable services. HR, having to access potential hires information, could be sent to an infected website as a result, causing their computer to be hacked. Ideally, HR will not have access to patient files and thus would not cause a bigger issue. Ensuring HR sticks to known, reputable hiring websites will be the best defence.
